Vancouver is one of Canada's most tech-savvy cities — and one of its priciest places to live. When you're paying Vancouver's sky-high rents, spending $140/month on a Shaw or Telus cable bundle feels particularly painful. That's why IPTV in Vancouver is growing faster than any other major Canadian city, with thousands of Metro Vancouver residents making the switch every month.

Whether you live in downtown Vancouver, North Shore, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, or Coquitlam, IPTV Canada delivers every Vancouver Canucks game on Sportsnet Pacific, all BC sports, local channels, and the city's diverse international content — at a fraction of cable TV costs.

Why Vancouver is Going IPTV

Vancouver's cord-cutting movement is driven by several local factors:

  • Tech-savvy population: Vancouver's large tech sector means residents are comfortable with streaming technology and quick to adopt new platforms.
  • High cost of living: Every dollar saved on cable goes a long way when rent is CA$2,500+ for a one-bedroom. IPTV saves CA$80–$110/month compared to Shaw or Telus TV.
  • Diverse international communities: Metro Vancouver is one of the most multicultural regions in Canada. Large communities from China, India, the Philippines, Korea, and Hong Kong need international channels that Canadian cable packages don't carry at a reasonable price.
  • Excellent fibre internet: Telus PureFibre and Shaw Fibre+ cover most of Metro Vancouver with gigabit speeds. IPTV performs flawlessly on these networks.

Vancouver Canucks — All IPTV Channels

Canucks fans, here's exactly where to find every game on IPTV Canada:

Sportsnet Pacific — The Home of the Canucks

Sportsnet Pacific broadcasts the majority of Vancouver Canucks regular season games. This is the West Coast's dedicated Sportsnet feed, providing local commentary, pre-game shows, and post-game analysis focused on BC sports. IPTV Canada includes Sportsnet Pacific (along with all other Sportsnet regional feeds: East, Ontario, West, and 360) in every subscription plan.

TSN Channels for Canucks Games

When games fall under TSN's broadcast rights (particularly mid-week national games and some playoff matchups), the Canucks appear on TSN 1, 2, or 4. IPTV Canada carries all five TSN channels, so you're covered no matter which network has the broadcast.

CBC for Playoff Hockey

When the Canucks make a playoff run, Saturday night games appear on CBC's Hockey Night in Canada. IPTV Canada includes CBC's national feed so you can watch every playoff moment with the iconic HNIC production values Vancouver fans love.

Vancouver Fan Pro Tip

The Canucks play 41 home games and 41 away games per season. With IPTV Canada, you catch every one — home games on Sportsnet Pacific and away games on the opposing team's regional Sportsnet or TSN feed. Never miss a road game again.

BC Sports: Lions, Whitecaps & More

The Canucks aren't the only game in town. IPTV Canada covers all of BC's major sports teams:

BC Lions (CFL)

BC Lions games air on TSN (various channels) during the CFL regular season and playoffs. IPTV Canada includes all TSN channels, so you'll catch every Lions game from opening kickoff to the Grey Cup. The Lions' West Division playoff runs are must-watch TV for Vancouver fans.

Vancouver Whitecaps FC (MLS)

Whitecaps matches are broadcast on OneSoccer (for MLS matches) and TSN for select playoff games. IPTV Canada includes OneSoccer, giving Vancouver soccer fans complete coverage of every MLS match, including the thrilling playoff pushes in the Western Conference.

Vancouver Giants (WHL)

For junior hockey fans, Vancouver Giants Western Hockey League games are available through regional sports feeds included in IPTV Canada. The WHL is the top development league for future Canucks stars.

Multicultural Channels for Metro Vancouver

Metro Vancouver is home to one of the largest and most diverse Asian communities in North America. IPTV Canada serves these communities with an unmatched international channel lineup:

Chinese-Language Channels (Most Requested in Vancouver)

  • CCTV-4, CGTN — Mainland Chinese national broadcasting
  • Phoenix TV, TVB — Popular Cantonese-language Hong Kong channels
  • TVBS, SET TV, EBC — Taiwanese channels for Vancouver's Taiwanese community
  • Fairchild TV (CHNM) — Vancouver's own Chinese-language channel

South Asian Channels (Large Punjabi Community in Surrey/Delta)

  • Zee TV, Sony LIV, Star Plus — Hindi entertainment
  • PTC Punjabi, Zee Punjabi — For Metro Vancouver's large Punjabi-speaking community
  • Star Sports — Cricket coverage essential for South Asian communities

Filipino Channels

  • GMA Pinoy TV, TFC — Top Filipino channels for Metro Vancouver's Filipino community

Internet Providers in Vancouver for IPTV Streaming

Vancouver is fortunate to have several excellent internet options perfect for IPTV:

  • Telus PureFibre: The gold standard for IPTV in Vancouver. Telus's 100% fibre network covers most of Metro Vancouver with symmetrical gigabit speeds. Even their entry 25 Mbps plan handles 4K IPTV perfectly.
  • Shaw Fibre+: Strong cable/fibre hybrid network with excellent coverage across Metro Vancouver. Plans from 150 Mbps to 1.5 Gbps. Handles 4K IPTV and multiple simultaneous streams easily.
  • Novus Fibre (Downtown Vancouver): Premium fibre ISP for downtown and inner-city Vancouver. Gigabit speeds, excellent customer service, competitive pricing.
  • Start / EBOX: Budget-friendly independent ISPs reselling Shaw infrastructure. Ideal for cost-conscious cord-cutters who want to maximize IPTV savings.
